RECON CAM · V4

Automatic outlining: Enable the camera, then tap OUTLINE once. The free AI model automatically identifies a limited set of visible objects and draws colored lines around their silhouettes. It runs continuously, without tapping the objects. On initial use, the AI model must download over the internet.

Green: people and everyday objects. Yellow: animals. Red: vehicles. These colors do not measure risk, threat, or safety. You can also choose one outline color for every detected shape.

Limits: This model cannot reliably recognize weapons, cash, coins, phones, license plates, credit cards, text, exits, or every object. It may miss partly hidden or very small objects. No model identifies threats or measures actual heat here. Model processing may be slow on an iPhone.

Distance: MARK OBJECT requires two taps for the separate rough distance estimator only. It does not control automatic outlines. Thermal and night are simulated filters, not sensors. Digital zoom cannot add optical detail.

Privacy: Video frames are processed on your device by this implementation, though the code and AI model are downloaded from external servers. Use screenshots only with others’ privacy in mind.
