CAMERA

iPhone controls camera permissions. Not all browsers expose the ultra-wide lens or hardware zoom.

Keeping the picture live takes priority over AI speed. If it freezes, use ↻. High detail uses more resources and may stall older iPhones.

READ WRITING

Automatic reading is OFF by default to reduce freezes. Tap READ TEXT below the camera to scan words manually. Text is kept away from the picture. Automatic speech needs a user tap to begin on iPhone.

ITEM CHECK

CHECK ITEM takes a detailed crop from the center and examines two orientations with the standard model. It works without the large weapon download. Conflicting knife, scissors, toothbrush or utensil guesses are shown as uncertain, not silently changed into knives. Scores measure model output, not the chance an identification is correct. For another experimental check, enable the optional model and use SCAN CENTER CLOSE-UP. It compares knife prompts with non-weapon lookalikes too.

IMPORTANT LIMITS

Object and text recognition need model downloads and may not work without internet on first load. This is a visual aid, not a security, medical, or emergency detection system. No camera can be permanently authorized by a web page; Safari controls permissions. Machine vision, night and false-color modes are visual filters, not infrared or thermal sensors.
