RECON CAM · HOW TO USE

1. Enable camera and allow Safari access. Use the modes and zoom slider. Thermal and night modes are color/brightness effects, not heat or infrared sensors.

2. For a distance estimate, tap MARK OBJECT, enter the object's known height, then tap the top and bottom of it. The app estimates range from apparent image height and an assumed camera field of view.

3. Tap PHOTO to save a snapshot. GPS and compass are optional.

Accuracy: Camera-only long-range readings are very approximate, especially after zoom or with an unknown object size. They are not military-grade or suitable for navigation, targeting, or safety decisions.
