RECON CAM · HOW TO USE

1. Enable camera and allow Safari access. Use the modes and zoom slider. Thermal and night modes are color/brightness effects, not heat or infrared sensors.

OBJECT OUTLINE: Tap OUTLINE to load AI shape segmentation. Once loaded, supported objects are traced along their silhouettes, not with boxes or generic edge highlights. Tap an object in the view to isolate it; tap SHOW ALL to restore all recognized shapes. AUTO SHAPES recognizes some common people, animals, vehicles and other everyday objects. TAP TO TRACE uses another free model to outline a selected object, including objects the app cannot identify by name. Tap the object, then use LIVE VIEW to resume. READ TEXT uses on-device OCR to identify visible words and numbers (such as EXIT signs and license plates), but may return incorrect characters. OCR highlights text regions, not precise individual letter outlines. Models require internet to download.

OBJECT TYPES: Phones, computers and electronics, weapons, money, coins, credit cards, license plates, doors and exits can be tapped for approximate shape tracing if their boundaries are visible. Do not rely on Recon Cam to identify weapons, exits, credentials, cash, or license plates accurately or automatically. Sensitive numbers are masked in the OCR display.

2. For a distance estimate, tap MARK OBJECT, enter the object's known height, then tap the top and bottom of it. The app estimates range from apparent image height and an assumed camera field of view.

3. Tap PHOTO to save a snapshot. GPS and compass are optional.

Accuracy: Camera-only long-range readings are very approximate, especially after zoom or with an unknown object size. They are not military-grade or suitable for navigation, targeting, or safety decisions.
